Check-in at the door

Every player checks in for every session. Parents do it on their phone.

What parents do

  1. Scan the QR code on the "Check in" sign at the door (or open phaseplayhoops.com/check-in).
  2. Tap their player's name. The session happening now is shown first, split by group.
  3. First check-in only: read and agree to the liability release and the photo/video media release, and type their name to sign. Both are required to check in.
  4. Take a quick photo of their player, then tap Check in.

What coaches do

  • Welcome the kids as they come in.
  • Shoot around with them before the session starts.
  • Be friendly. First impressions set the tone for the day.

What Coach Michael does

  • Makes sure every player is checked in, and welcomes campers at the door.
  • Handles any check-in problem: a name missing from the list, a parent without a phone, or releases not signed. Send those parents to Coach Michael.

Coach instructions

How each block runs

  1. Coach Michael brings the players together, demonstrates the drill and answers questions.
  2. He then releases the players to two baskets, each run by coaches.
  3. Coaches run their basket: rules, rotations, general basketball coaching and suggestions to players.

The camp moves fast, and directions are kept simple to follow. Numbered cones mark where everything goes on the court.

Court setups

Numbered cones mark every spot.

1. The 12-spot grid: Free Play / Teach Locations

Full court, 12 numbered spots per half, mirrored across midcourt. The coach stands at the midcourt sideline. Use the grid to teach locations in Free Play and to relocate players on 2 whistles.

2. 900 Line: "RIP 2-FOOT FINISHING"

Entries, sweeps and 2-foot finishes run from the 900 Line at each end.

3. Live Defense starts: Defend the Trend, 11:15

Then: Closeout / Gap / Hand Position (11:25-11:30), High Shot Advantage 2v2 and 3v3, 3v3 with a good outlet, and 3v3 or 4v4 Continuous (FIBA) to close the live block.

Whistle system

1 whistle = EyesEveryone stops, eyes on the coach. No talking, no ball movement.
2 whistles = RelocateThe coach names the spot out loud, for example "relocate to the far baseline" or "relocate to Station 3." Players move on the second whistle to the spot named.

Coach rules: every block, every coach

  • Name everything. Every drill gets a name.
  • Drill names in ALL CAPS with quotations, for example "DEFENSE FOOTWORK". Sequence steps are numbered with italic names, for example 1 Hip Switch, 2 Drop & Hop, 3 Drop, Hop & Recover.
  • Before we break, Coach Michael names the drill and everybody repeats it. Call and response, then break to start.
  • Neat lines. Players line up in neat lines.
  • Open for questions before we break. The coach opens the floor before players break.
