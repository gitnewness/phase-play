    Phase 1

    Wiring to Win

    Skills on their own look great in training, but connecting them in sequences and conditions patches the connection between skills and performance on the court. Most players have great skills, but have never wired those skills to win.

    Phase 2

    Defend the Trend

    Defense is more than "wanting it more" or having an "iron will". Learn skills and techniques that are used by lockdown defenders and gain confidence on the floor without scoring a point.

    Phase 3

    Chatter that Matters

    Chatter that matters. Communication with teammates on and off the floor is a critical piece of player development. Learn cues navigating the floor and being a great teammate in this foundational phase of the game.

    Phase 4

    Gameplay & Integration

    The Challenge

    The gap between skill building and gameplay can be patched with accountability and live coaching. Some players can hold themselves accountable as soon as they learn about the change or improvement while others need a separate pair of eyes, such as a coach or a parent to help.

    The Solution

    A recorded live hour game can help a player view their movement their decision-making and presence on the court in a live game with real refs. Some players have never seen themselves play on video and one thing about video is it does not lie. The recorded video will be available to all campers through media download while video breakdown is available in specific packages and also add-ons up to a week after the camp.

    Phase 4: Just Play & Watch Later

    Strictly gameplay with licensed referees & full stats with value point breakdown.

    Tuesday is when the players put it all together in gameplay. Teams will be split up into even teams and peer coached as they navigate the game with their new communication skills to integrate the skills they learned from Day 1.

    Upon completion of the game day, we will stat the games and deliver both the full game footage with stats and also a value point breakdown of their production on the court. Value points are a weighted formula that values more actions than just traditional stats. This can help players and parents understand how they are contributing to their teams and how to best assess their strengths and weaknesses in gameplay.

    To further understand their presence on the court, live video breakdown is available with further coaching, play by play assessment and steps to improve game plans. Please see Breakdown add-on below.
